# Demo walkthrough script: Insight Factories

A short screen recording of the open demo, about 4 minutes. The data is synthetic (fictional marketplace Harbor Market, generated by `pipeline/generate.mjs`), so the script names screens and gestures, not numbers. Read the numbers from the screen when recording.

## Story

One story instead of a tour of screens. The AI assistant answers questions about Auto data poorly. The BI partner of the Cars subdomain opens the portal and walks the path: where we are → why → what is broken → what to do → how to involve people.

Cast: the company → the Auto domain → the Cars subdomain (the bottleneck is one readiness component). Contrast: a healthier subdomain in the same domain, for example Vehicle History.

## Format

- 1440×900. A slow zoom on the area the caption talks about. Each screen holds at least 4 seconds, key screens 6–8 seconds.
- Caption: a dark strip across the bottom, one or two lines of plain text. The act number and name sit small above the text.
- Act dividers: a 2-second title card with the act number, name and one line on what we will see.
- Theme: record in one theme (light or dark) for the whole video.

| # | Screen | Action | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | Title card | — | Insight Factories — observability for domain data governance |
| 0.2 | Card "Tasks", items appear one by one | — | Five tasks: state, AI readiness, causes, action, engagement |
| 0.3 | Home `#/`, wide shot | zoom on the AI-Ready score tile | One story: why the assistant answers questions about Auto data poorly |

Card "Tasks":

1. **Show the state of domain data** — health of data marts, metrics and dashboards, freshness, incidents, cost.
2. **Measure AI readiness** — the AI-Ready score from two axes, levels L0–L4, rank among domains.
3. **Explain the cause** — components with fact and target, links and flow from data mart to dashboard.
4. **Lead to action** — tasks with their effect on the score and a link to the tool where the fix happens.
5. **Engage** — the factory, the Insight Factories world, monthly awards.

## Say once

- All data is synthetic; numbers change with the generator seed.
- The game layer is optional: everything in 3D is also available in the portal tables.
- The AI-Ready score is the minimum of readiness and result, so improving only one axis does not raise it.
