# Insight Factories — functional blocks

Specification of the open demo: the portal (`app/index.html`), the factory page (`app/domain.html`), the Insight Factories world (`app/world.html`) and the links window (`app/graph.html`).

All data in the demo is synthetic. It describes the fictional clinic network Harbor Health and is generated by `pipeline/generate.mjs` into `app/data/`. Where this spec says "source", it names the module of the synthetic data set and the kind of production system that would feed it in a real deployment.

Terms: a **factory** is a subdomain (the 3D page `domain.html` and a building in the world), a **workshop** is a building on the factory page (a group of data marts), an **industrial zone** is a domain in the world.

Priority: **P0** — core, the product does not exist without it; **P1** — engagement and growth; **P2** — showcase; **P3** — decoration.

How to read: each block has a code, purpose, data, requirements (must / nice to have), a definition of done and one or two references — the best examples of this function in products or games. Status: **done** — works in the demo; **partial** — works, but part of the data is labeled "sample" or some requirements are missing; **prototype** — a first version on sample data; **stub** — a mockup with a description, no logic; **planned** — not built.

## Cross-cutting principles

They apply to every block and are not repeated in the requirements.

1. **The portal shows state, it does not change it.** Every action leads to the tool where the work is done: Semantic Layer, Dashboards, Data Catalog, Git, Knowledge Base. The exception is the registry of domains, subdomains and roles: the portal keeps it as the Domain Registry. The portal keeps no other records or statuses of its own.
2. **One calculation per indicator.** Health comes from SLO telemetry, admission from object metadata, certification from the BI partner's decision, cost from infrastructure billing. The three signals are never merged into one score. If the source tool already shows a status (for example, dashboard health in the BI tool), the portal shows that status and does not recompute it.
3. **Provenance is always visible.** Every number has a snapshot date or a period. Data without a source is labeled "sample" and does not count in rankings or points.
4. **One engine, different skins.** `index.html`, `portal.js`, `model.js`, `products.js` and `portal.css` form a shared engine. Terms, roles, task texts and links to the 3D pages live only in `skin.js`.
5. **3D is a mode, not the base.** Everything on the map and in the factory is available in the portal without 3D. The 3D pages open as separate pages, not in an iframe.
6. **People are fictional.** Names in `data/names.js` are generated. In a real deployment, personal names stay inside the company network; for external showings roles are replaced by job titles.

## A. Shell

### A1. Shell and navigation
- **Purpose.** A constant frame for the portal: where I am, where I can go, how to get back.
- **Data.** Catalog of domains and subdomains from `model.js`.
- **Must.**
  - Side menu: AI readiness with the function goal, the 3D world, Health, Admission and Certification with queue sizes, Answers, My factories, Digest, Onboarding; list of domains with a health color and the number of subdomains.
  - Breadcrumbs on every page; the page address in the URL (`#/d/…`, `#/s/…/tab`) so a link can be shared.
  - Esc closes a panel, the browser back button returns to the previous screen.
  - Width from 1280 px without horizontal scroll; at 980 px the menu collapses.
- **Nice to have.** Keyboard shortcuts for sections; remembering the last opened cabinet.
- **Done when** any screen opens by a direct link and one click leads one level up.
- **References.**
  - [Backstage Software Catalog](https://backstage.io/docs/features/software-catalog/) — entity catalog with tabs on the entity page; the same "catalog → cabinet" model.
  - [Linear](https://linear.app) — dense, minimal keyboard navigation, a model of a calm interface.

### A2. Global search
- **Purpose.** Find a domain, subdomain, object or person in a couple of seconds.
- **Data.** Names of domains, subdomains and objects; people from `names.js`.
- **Must.**
  - Substring search from 2 characters, results grouped: subdomains, objects, people.
  - Each result shows type, subdomain and health status; a click opens the cabinet or the object panel.
  - Response under 100 ms on the full catalog; `/` focuses the search.
- **Nice to have.** Fuzzy search; search by object owner.
- **Done when** any object with a health score is found by a fragment of its name.
- **References.**
  - [Linear](https://linear.app) — Cmd+K command palette: search and navigation in one window.
  - [DataHub](https://datahub.com/products/) — search across all data types with facets by domain and type.

### A3. Mode switch
- **Purpose.** The same subdomain or domain can be viewed as tables (cabinet) or in 3D (factory, map), switching without losing context.
- **Must.**
  - The subdomain cabinet has a "Cabinet / Factory / On the map" switch; the domain cabinet has "Cabinet / On the map".
  - Every 3D page links back to the cabinet of the same subdomain or domain.
  - A map opened from a cabinet focuses on that domain or subdomain.
- **Done when** the loop "cabinet → factory → map → cabinet" works without searching again.
- **References.**
  - [Celonis Process Explorer](https://www.celonis.com/) — detail slider: from the main flow to individual variants on the same screen.
  - [Factorio](https://factorio.com) — map view: a seamless switch from the detailed view to the overview.

## B. Platform overview

### B1. AI readiness
- **Purpose.** The home page: how ready the company's data is for AI answers under the 2027 method, and what limits it.
- **Data.** Signals (`data/signals.js`), objects (`data/data.js`), usage and cost; scoring in `model.js` (`score4`, `drivers`, `counters`, `FUNC`).
- **Must.**
  - Function goal: the mean AI-Ready score of subdomains, with the number of subdomains above 90 next to it. The year result is the mean of the three Q4 windows.
  - Shares on Tier 0–2 objects: healthy by SLO, passing admission, certified; SLO breaches; sandbox objects left out.
  - Metric tree in six sections: Data, Meaning, Trust, Answer, Loop, Counter-metrics. P0 cards carry the label "in the minimum" or "by readiness", the fact, the target and the attainment; P1 drivers and counter-metrics sit under them. A click opens the queue behind the card.
  - Readiness of the two P0 that join later: three automatic conditions (28 days without gaps, 80% of objects tagged, baseline published) for each.
  - Score distribution in ten-point buckets, the P0 that limits each subdomain, levels L0–L4.
  - Bubble chart: x is the weaker of AI-ready data marts and semantic coverage (attainment), y is the golden set pass rate with the threshold line, size is usage.
  - Domain table: score (mean of subdomains), subdomains above 90, P0 facts pooled over the domain, SLO, admission, certification, level.
- **Done when** the function goal on the page equals the mean of the subdomain scores shown in the cabinets.
- **References.**
  - [Microsoft Purview Data Governance Report](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/unified-catalog-reports-data-governance) — a short set of controls broken down by domain.
  - [Monte Carlo Data Reliability Dashboard](https://docs.getmontecarlo.com/docs/using-data-product-dashboards) — reliability over time: incidents, time to resolve, breakdown by domain.

### B2. Domain ranking
- **Purpose.** Compare domains and see who is behind.
- **Must.**
  - Table: health with trend, metrics, data marts, dashboards, objects, problems, CPU and CPU per healthy object, good output, AI-Ready.
  - Sorting by any column; a click opens the domain cabinet.
  - Domains without objects are shown and labeled, not hidden.
- **Not yet in the demo.** A "Problems" column and sorting by a click on the header.
- **Nice to have.** Rank and its change over 4 weeks; filter by business unit.
- **Done when** the table sorts by any column and the totals match the domain cabinets.
- **References.**
  - [Purview: data health by governance domain](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/unified-catalog-reports-data-governance) — controls for each domain side by side.
  - [OpsLevel Rubric](https://docs.opslevel.com/docs/categories-and-levels) — maturity levels by team in one table.

### B3. Problem registry
- **Replaced.** The address `#/hot` opens the Health queue (G6). Most old "problems" were admission failures such as undescribed columns; they now live in Admission (G7).
- **Purpose.** Show what to fix first: popular objects that fail the health score.
- **Data.** Health score: status and popularity; the threshold is the 75th percentile within the object type.
- **Must.**
  - Platform-wide list with a type filter; sorting by popularity normalized by type (dashboards do not crowd out metrics).
  - Each row shows subdomain, status, number of uses; a click opens the object card.
  - The problem badge (a triangle with a number) looks the same in every block.
- **Nice to have.** List export.
- **In the demo.** Each row shows the cause (the first failed check; if all visible checks pass, "other health score components"), "Affected downstream" by lineage and a link to the tool where it is fixed. Filters: type, domain, My factories. Sorting: by downstream consumers, then by uses.
- **Done when** the definition of a problem is the same in the portal, on the map and in the factory, and the counts match.
- **References.**
  - [Monte Carlo](https://docs.getmontecarlo.com/docs/using-data-product-dashboards) — incidents prioritized by impact on data products.
  - [Euno](https://euno.ai/) — dashboard usage is propagated up the lineage to tables: priority by real consumption.

### B4. My factories
- **Purpose.** The entry point for a BI partner: only their subdomains.
- **Data.** Domain Registry (roles), `names.js`, current user.
- **Must.**
  - Cards of subdomains where the user holds any role, with the role label.
  - An empty state explaining how to get into the Domain Registry.
- **In the demo.** A table of own subdomains: role, health with trend, level, problems, late data marts, open incidents, main action; a link to the weekly digest preview (G5).
- **References.**
  - [Backstage](https://backstage.io/docs/features/software-catalog/) — "Owned by me" filter in the catalog.
  - [Cortex Scorecards](https://docs.cortex.io/standardize/scorecards) — a personal list of entities with their level.

### C9. Domain and subdomain infracost
- An "Infracost" tab in the subdomain cabinet (`#/s/<i>/infra`): CPU and the share of the domain's CPU, disk, CPU per healthy object, savings, CPU of data marts and metrics, savings groups, the most expensive data marts and metrics.

### C10. Domain creation and the Domain Registry (stub)
- The portal keeps the Domain Registry: domains, subdomains and roles (data owner, BI partner, metrics curator, AI champion) are stored and changed here.
- Screen `#/new` (the "+ Create" link next to the domain list): name, business unit, data owner, BI partner, subdomains, object attribution rules, Control Plane workspace. Submitting builds a request on screen and sends nothing.
- To make it work:
  - registry storage and a write API in the portal;
  - consumers read the registry: the health score snapshot build, the Semantic Layer, the digest bot, Control Plane workspaces;
  - object attribution rules are set in the portal and passed to the snapshot build;
  - approval in the portal: the data owner and the head of BI confirm the request; change history is kept in the registry.

### C11. BI partner onboarding
- An "Explore the factory" block: a guided tour of the factory (`domain.html?d=<i>&tour=1`: 14 stops — raw materials, workshops, defects, certified data marts, metric warehouses, shipping, conveyors, office, ground indicators, suppliers, consumers, panel, what next; arrow keys and Esc), the world, the cabinet, the links window; section videos are a stub.
- Page `#/onboarding/<subdomain>` in the "Onboarding" menu: 9 steps with statuses from portal data — role in the Domain Registry, staff, data mart owners, business checkers, key metrics, dashboards on healthy data, Knowledge Base skill, golden set, digest subscription; progress and a link to an onboarding checklist in the Wiki.
- Stubs: manual step check-off and the digest subscription need stored onboarding state and a Chat bot; the golden set is generated.

### D2. Self-service AI
- **Purpose.** How ready the domain is for questions to an AI analyst and how well the AI answers.
- **Data.** Knowledge Base readiness (FAQ, glossary, text-to-SQL examples, described objects, lineage) from `data/data.js`; AI usage among non-analysts from `data/o3.js`. Labeled "sample": AI Assistant conversations, tool calls, accuracy on the golden set, frequent questions.
- **Must.**
  - Knowledge Base readiness indicators and links to where it is filled in (the domain's Knowledge Base in Git).
  - Accuracy on the golden set of questions and regressions between runs.
  - Question coverage by healthy metrics: the share of questions answered with a healthy Semantic Layer metric.
  - The golden set used for measurement is kept separate from the verified queries that answers rely on.
- **Nice to have.** A list of questions without an answer on trusted semantics — a direct source of tasks for the metrics curator.
- **Open in a real deployment.** AI Assistant conversations have to be logged and attributed to the domain of the question, not to the department of the person asking; a source of verified queries has to be defined.
- **Done when** every indicator in the block is computed from logs, with no "sample" labels.
- **References.**
  - [Databricks Genie Benchmarks](https://www.databricks.com/blog/operationalizing-genie-ontology-your-data-stack) — benchmark questions kept apart from the model context, a run after every change.

### D10. Glossary as a knowledge graph
- **Purpose.** The subdomain's business terms with definitions and sources, linked to Semantic Layer metrics and dimensions, data marts and columns. An agent and an analyst understand what a word in a question means and which object computes it.
- **Data.** In a real deployment: candidates are extracted by an LLM from selected Wiki spaces with quotes; approved terms and links live in a glossary store; metrics and dimensions come from the Semantic Layer; data marts and columns from the Data Catalog; health from the health score. In the demo the terms are derived from frequent words in object names and the rest is generated and labeled "sample".
- **Must.**
  - A "Glossary" tab (`#/s/<i>/gl`) after the metrics tab; the counter shows approved terms, with the number in review next to it.
  - A list with search by name, synonyms and hidden labels; filters by status and key terms.
  - Term card: definition, synonyms, owner, status, sources with a quote and a link to the page, related concepts, metrics and data marts with health, edit history.
  - A focus-mode graph: the term in the center, neighbors 1–2 steps away, at most 40 nodes; nodes are term, metric, dimension, data mart, column, document; an approved link is solid, a suggested one is dashed.
  - Term provenance is visible: from the Wiki (model, date), manual (author), import; LLM candidates without review are not visible to agents and do not count toward the AI-Ready score.
  - The empty state explains how to connect sources and which metrics to start with.
- **Nice to have.** A review mode with keyboard shortcuts, merging duplicates, definition conflicts, staleness when the source page changes; "Concept map" and "Coverage" modes; a `resolve` API and agent tools; a contribution to 1.4 Domain knowledge through coverage of used metrics and data marts by approved terms.
- **Done when** every approved term has a source, the subdomain's key metrics have a term, and an agent asked a question with a glossary word picks the linked metric.
- **All domain terms in 3D** (`glmap3d.js`): term spheres of all subdomains in the domain; size is the number of linked metrics and data marts, color is status; rotate, zoom, click to select (several allowed), double-click opens the term. Links between selected terms are labeled by kind: "broader" (hierarchy), "includes · N" (objects of one term are almost all inside another), "N shared" (shared metrics and data marts), "synonym".

- **First choice.** Objects of a term have a "set as first choice" star: the object agents and text-to-SQL take first; such objects are listed first.
- **Certification.** A crown in the term's object list, in object tables (Governance column) and in the object card: certify and revoke; in the demo this is a draft in page memory.
- **Term editing.** From the card and from the 3D map: name, definition, example, synonyms, broader term, related terms, key flag; saving sends a draft to review.
- **Details.** [Glossary design](glossary-kg-design.md).

### G4. AI-Ready score and levels L0–L4
- **Purpose.** One system for the AI readiness of a subdomain under the 2027 method (AI-Ready v4).
- **Model.** Score of a subdomain = min of attainment of the P0 metrics in the minimum, over a 28-day window, on Tier 0–2 objects. Attainment = min(100%, fact / target). A P0 without objects of its type is not counted. A domain score is the mean of its subdomain scores; P0 facts of a domain are pooled over its objects.
- **P0 in the minimum.**
  - **Consumption of AI-ready data marts** — share of data mart consumption (reads plus downstream dashboard users) on data marts that pass admission, are healthy by SLO and are certified. Target 30%.
  - **Measures covered by healthy metrics** — share of key measures in consumed data marts and report datasets that a healthy Semantic Layer metric represents, weighted by consumption. Target 50%. Synthetic in `SEM`.
  - **Correct answers on the golden set** — 50+ questions, removed from the pack during the run. A threshold: 60% in Q1–Q2, 80% from Q3. No golden set of 50+ questions counts as 0%.
- **P0 by readiness.** Shown next to the score with the label "by readiness"; they join the minimum from the quarter after their three readiness conditions hold (`READY`).
  - **Correct answers on real questions** — held-out monthly sample from traces, threads and requests; target is growth from the baseline (baseline + 10 pp in the demo); fewer than 20 questions shows no value.
  - **Views of trusted reports** — share of report views on reports that are certified, healthy and admitted. Target 45%.
- **Counter-metrics, outside the minimum.** AI use by non-analysts (proxy for JTBD penetration, `data/o3.js`); useful load per CPU hour — uses of AI-ready objects per CPU hour (hypothesis).
- **Levels.** L0 — a BI partner or a data owner is missing; L1 — both roles and at least 70% of Tier 0–2 objects pass admission; L2 — golden set of 50+ questions and a published knowledge pack skill; L3 — golden set above the threshold and every P0 in the minimum at target except one; L4 — every P0 in the minimum at target. A domain shows the median level of its subdomains.
- **Where in the portal.** Subdomain overview: level, score and the P0 that sets it, next-level rules, P0 rows, counter-metrics, then Work by role (G10). The "AI-Ready Score" tab: the metric tree for this subdomain, how each P0 is computed, comparison chart.
- **Tasks.** The gain of a task is the change of the minimum after the task moves its P0 fact; a task that does not touch the weakest P0 shows no effect.
- **Limits of the demo.** Golden set, held-out sample, feedback and semantic coverage are generated; weekly score history is an estimate that follows the healthy share of data marts.
- **References.**
  - [OpsLevel](https://docs.opslevel.com/docs/categories-and-levels) — levels in strict order with conditions for the next level.
  - [Cortex Scorecards](https://docs.cortex.io/standardize/scorecards) — rules grouped into levels with progress per entity.
